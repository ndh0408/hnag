## SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM

**Headline:** *Vietnamese people waste **23 minutes a day** deciding what to eat.*

**Visual:** Split screen — 5 phone screens side-by-side (Grab, Shopee, Be, TikTok, Google Maps), all open, person looking exhausted.

**3 stats (large numbers, animated count-up):**
```
   23 min          $42 B                67%
   per day,        Vietnam food         Gen Z say they
   per person      market 2026          "hate deciding"
```

**The decision is broken because:**
1. ❌ Fragmented across 4–5 apps
2. ❌ No personalization (one-size-fits-all)
3. ❌ Group conflict ("anh chọn đi" — "em chọn đi")
4. ❌ 35% fridge ingredients wasted
5. ❌ Trends on TikTok ≠ where to actually eat them

**Speaker note (45s):** *"Open your phone. Look at how you decide what to eat. You bounce between GrabFood, Shopee, TikTok, Google Maps, your group chat. You spend 20 minutes scrolling. You settle for the same 5 places. You wasted that one piece of fish you bought yesterday. And when you're with friends? Multiply that pain by N people."*
